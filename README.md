# Twilight Descent

> **A dark-fantasy RPG expansion for Minecraft Bedrock Edition.**
> Explore forgotten lands, discover unusual materials, uncover ancient mysteries, and survive a world that becomes more dangerous the farther you venture.

**Development status:** Work in progress — biome and world-generation development.

Twilight Descent is being built around exploration, atmosphere, risk, and progression. The goal is not just to add new blocks or make the Overworld look different: every region should eventually have its own identity, resources, inhabitants, discoveries, and reasons to return.

## 🌍 What We're Working On Now

Our current priority is building a consistent foundation for the **Overworld biomes** before expanding into larger RPG systems. Much of the terrain, foliage, and feature generation is in active testing; the visuals and distribution are still being adjusted.

- **Biome terrain:** Improve size, rarity, transitions, and appropriate placement among vanilla biomes.
- **Natural environments:** Finish custom ground blocks, stone layers, trees, grass, flowers, mushrooms, reeds, and hanging vegetation.
- **World generation:** Refine patches, shorelines, cave floors, pools, waterfalls, and other natural features.
- **Visual consistency:** Create readable Minecraft-style textures, with controlled color palettes and subtler highlights.
- **Performance and compatibility:** Test generation in new worlds and check that features do not overcrowd or replace unrelated terrain.

## 🌳 Custom Trees & Vegetation — Coming Next

We are designing distinct tree families instead of relying on recolored vanilla trees. The planned sets include:

- **Prismbark Tree** — Prismward Glade
- **Veilwillow Tree** — Veilmoss Hollow
- **Tufabloom Tree** — Azure Tufa Springs
- **Roseglass Scrub Tree** — Roseglass Strand
- **Mirewillow Tree** — Sunken Vale
- **Dreadmangrove Tree** — Umbral Marsh

Additional work includes growth variants, saplings, fallen logs, hanging moss, vines, bushes, flowers, and biome-specific undergrowth. Leaf textures and tree shapes remain subject to redesign and gameplay testing.

## ✨ Future Systems

### Crystals and Materials

A larger crystal ecosystem is planned, with growth stages, special harvesting, rare variants, and reactions to events or nearby activity. The proposed material families include **Prismite, Charged Prismite, Aetherite, Veilrite, Sylphrite, Bloomwake, Starvein, Gleamore, and Aurorite**. Ores, deep-stone variants, raw materials, and crafting uses will be refined as progression takes shape.

### Creatures, Enemies, and Bosses

Biomes should eventually have their own wildlife, ambient life, threats, and rare encounters. Concepts under consideration include the **Prismwing Sylph**, crystal creatures such as **Shardlings**, and more imposing enemies tied to corrupted regions. Unique minibosses and bosses are planned for later development.

### RPG Progression and Combat

Longer-term plans include player progression, exploration rewards, equipment tiers, improved encounters, and meaningful light-versus-dark choices. The goal is a challenging dark-fantasy adventure that works for solo play and multiplayer rather than a collection of unrelated mechanics.

### Structures, Dungeons, and Events

Look for ruined roads, abandoned shrines, groves, ancient landmarks, hidden caves, and eventually larger dungeon-like discoveries. A **Crystal Bloom** world event is one planned way to make certain regions change over time.

### Atmosphere

Distinct ambience, lighting, weather effects, particles, environmental sounds, and nighttime behavior will be added after the core terrain and vegetation are stable.

## 🛠️ Development Roadmap

- [ ] **Phase 1 — Biome foundations:** Finalize terrain, biome distribution, surface materials, stone/deep-stone generation, and shoreline transitions.
- [ ] **Phase 2 — Flora and trees:** Finish leaf textures, tree families, saplings, vegetation placement, and large natural landmarks.
- [ ] **Phase 3 — Underground environments:** Improve Luminous Glade caves, custom cave floors, giant mushrooms, pools, and mineral formations.
- [ ] **Phase 4 — Resources and crafting:** Complete ore families, material drops, recipes, harvesting rules, and equipment progression.
- [ ] **Phase 5 — Creatures and encounters:** Add biome wildlife, hostile variants, ambient entities, and combat encounters.
- [ ] **Phase 6 — Structures and adventure:** Introduce ruins, shrines, special structures, dungeons, and bosses.
- [ ] **Phase 7 — RPG mechanics:** Build progression, abilities, light/dark paths, and events into a cohesive gameplay loop.
- [ ] **Phase 8 — Polish and expansion:** Balance performance, improve audio/visual atmosphere, test multiplayer, and later consider the Nether, End, or other dimensions.

The checklist shows **work remaining**, not fixed release milestones. Features may change, move between phases, or be removed after testing.

## ⚠️ Alpha Development Notes

Twilight Descent is still evolving. Screenshots and previews may include experimental textures, placeholder vegetation, or unfinished generation. Biomes can be rebalanced, renamed, and redesigned as the world comes together. No public release date is announced in this README.
